# 🐕 Who Let the Dogs Out?

![Biscuit's shelter](images/case-cover.jpg)

*A missing shelter dog. Five data sources. One investigation.*

## Case Brief

**Case Type:** Missing Animal Investigation  
**Organization:** Fictional animal shelter  
**Primary Tools:** SQL, Excel, Power BI  
**Status:** Investigation in progress

### The Incident

Biscuit, a dog housed at the shelter, was discovered missing during the evening shift. Shelter staff confirmed that Biscuit had been present earlier in the day, but the precise time of disappearance has not been established.

Management has requested a data-driven investigation to reconstruct the timeline and identify potential gaps in the shelter's records.

### Investigative Objectives

1. Establish the most likely time window in which Biscuit went missing.
2. Identify staff members who were scheduled or recorded as entering relevant areas during that window.
3. Compare access logs against kennel checks and animal movement records.
4. Identify inconsistencies, missing records, and other relevant patterns.
5. Present findings and explain what the available evidence can and cannot establish.

### Planned Data Sources

- Employee shift schedules
- Key-card access logs
- Kennel inspection records
- Animal movement records

### Deliverables

- Documented datasets and data dictionary
- SQL queries and investigation notes
- Supporting analysis and visualizations
- Final findings and limitations

### Important Note

This is a fictional educational investigation. Presence at a location or a discrepancy in a record does not establish wrongdoing. Conclusions will be based on the available evidence and its limitations.

## Investigation Status

The case scenario has been established. Dataset design, data validation, and analysis are the next steps.
